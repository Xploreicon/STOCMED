# StocMed Competitive Positioning

## What changed

StocMed is not competing only with QuickBooks and Excel. Nigerian pharmacy owners are also sold combined pharmacy and supermarket POS products, including Tracepos, Stocker ERP, SwiftPOS, EliteTeQ, and CloudPOS. These products validate the need for one checkout and one inventory across medicines and front-store goods.

Expiry tracking alone is not a defensible claim. Competing POS products already market batch, expiry, prescription, and supermarket stock features, often from roughly N15,000 per month. Confirm current vendor pricing and customer-count claims before using them externally.

## The StocMed wedge

Lead with patient demand intelligence:

> 14 patients near you searched for Amlodipine this week and you were out of stock.

That signal connects local patient intent to the pharmacy's stock decisions. A conventional POS can report what sold; StocMed also shows what patients wanted but could not buy. The self-enriching medicine catalogue and medicine-only demand dataset make this signal stronger over time.

## Sales guidance

- Position unified Medicine + Store inventory as expected operational completeness, not the moat.
- Position FEFO, expiry exposure, procurement, and one mixed checkout as reasons a pharmacy does not need a second system.
- Position local unmet demand and patient discovery as the reason to choose StocMed over an established POS.
- Lead with free-to-start as the adoption wedge for pharmacies currently using paper, Excel, or another POS.
- Never imply that grocery or personal-care searches enter demand analytics. Store rows are tenant-private and structurally excluded.

## Objection handling

**"Tracepos already offered me this."**

They may already cover checkout and stock control. StocMed adds the patient side: nearby medicine searches, out-of-stock demand, verified pharmacy discovery, and a direct path from demand to replenishment.

**"We already track expiry."**

Keep that workflow. StocMed extends expiry visibility across medicines and perishables while tying medicine inventory to patient demand. Expiry control is operational value; demand intelligence is the differentiator.

**"We use QuickBooks."**

StocMed imports the inventory, keeps a ledger and QuickBooks-ready exports, then adds pharmacy-specific batch, expiry, FEFO, prescription safeguards, patient discovery, and unmet-demand signals.
